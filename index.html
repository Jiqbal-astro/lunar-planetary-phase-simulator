What the curve shows

A planet reflects starlight like the Moon: we see a thin crescent near transit (phase 0) and a full disk just before and after the secondary eclipse (phase 0.5), when it passes behind the star.

The flux ratio is Fp/F★ = Ag (Rp/r)² Φ(α). Because Rp/r is tiny, even a reflective hot Jupiter adds only tens of ppm. Earth-sized planets in the habitable zone add less than 0.1 ppm.
